P

Queen is the founder of Poised Pen, LLC. With 20+ years in AML, KYC, and BSA compliance — across banking, cannabis, and federal government work — she brings an insider's eye to resume writing, compliance templates, and operational guides built to hold up under real scrutiny.

Poised Pen was founded on a simple idea: the people best equipped to write a compliance resume or build a KYC template are the ones who have reviewed thousands of them. That experience runs through institutions including Fifth Third Bank, Citibank, and Trulieve, alongside time in federal government work.

That vantage point shapes everything Poised Pen produces: resumes written the way hiring managers in risk and compliance actually read them, and templates built to hold up against an examiner, not just look tidy in a folder.
